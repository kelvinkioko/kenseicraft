How to make an iPhone video smaller

Compress videos by up to 80%, tune resolution, frame rate and bitrate, and see the expected storage savings before you save or share the result.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Video Compressor: VideoShrink.

Step by step

  1. Choose a video and decide whether you need a smaller sharing copy or a higher-quality archive.
  2. Use a preset or adjust resolution, frame rate and bitrate in VideoShrink. Smaller output generally involves a quality trade-off.
  3. Save and play the compressed copy, checking motion and fine detail before sharing it or removing the original.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
