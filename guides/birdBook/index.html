How to keep a useful bird sighting collection

Build a birding collection, set spotting goals and explore species information.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Bird Book.

Step by step

  1. Record where and when you spotted the bird, its size, colors and behavior.
  2. Compare species information and calls, checking several distinguishing features.
  3. Save the sighting to your collection and review your spotting goals.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
