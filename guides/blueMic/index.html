How to use your iPhone with a Bluetooth speaker microphone

Connect to a Bluetooth speaker or headphones, speak through your iPhone in real time and shape the sound with voice filters, echo control and custom mic styles.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Bluetooth Speaker Mic: BlueMic.

Step by step

  1. Connect your iPhone to the Bluetooth speaker or headphones you want to use.
  2. Open BlueMic, allow microphone access when prompted and test speech at low speaker volume.
  3. Adjust the sound controls and keep the phone away from the speaker to reduce feedback. Bluetooth delay can vary by setup.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
