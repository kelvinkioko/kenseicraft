How to scan and organize documents as PDFs

Scan documents into PDFs, organize pages, merge files and share documents.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using PDF Scanner.

Step by step

  1. Place the document on a flat surface with even lighting and capture every page.
  2. Review the scan for readable text, correct page order and missing edges before saving.
  3. Merge or split pages as needed, then share the PDF and check that the recipient can open it.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
