03

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information from users of Kensei Craft. All data generated by the application, such as business details, client information, and invoices, is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

04

How we use your information

Since we do not collect any user information, we do not use your data for any purposes. The data you input into the application remains on your device and is under your control.

Legal basis

Since we do not process any user data, the legal bases outlined under the GDPR are not applicable to our operations.

Sharing your information

We do not share your personal data with any third parties.

05

Storage, security & retention

We do not store any of your personal data remotely. All data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for managing and backing up your data.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the application against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Since data is stored locally, we recommend following best practices for device security.

We do not retain your personal data. All data is managed and retained by you on your device.

06

Your data protection rights

Under the GDPR, you have certain rights regarding your personal data. Since we do not collect or process user data covered by this policy, these rights are not applicable in this context. You maintain full control over the data you input into the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the effective date above. You are advised to review this page periodically for changes.
