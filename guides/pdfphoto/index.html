How to turn a collection of photos into a PDF

Convert photos to PDF documents with custom layouts and paper sizes.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using PDF Photos.

Step by step

  1. Select the photos you want to include and put them in reading order.
  2. Choose a paper size and layout, then check that each photo fits without cutting off important details.
  3. Export the PDF, open it to inspect every page, and share the checked file.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
