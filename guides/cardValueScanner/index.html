How to identify and organize trading cards

Photograph a trading card to identify it, check current market estimates, follow price movement and keep your collection organised in one portfolio.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Card value scanner - MonValue.

Step by step

  1. Photograph the card clearly and record its name, set, number, language and visible condition.
  2. Use MonValue to identify the card and explore market estimates; check that the edition matches your card.
  3. Save it to your collection and compare recent sold examples in similar condition before deciding a price.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
