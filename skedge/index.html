How to plan assignments alongside your weekly schedule

  1. Add your fixed classes, work hours and appointments to a schedule.
  2. Break assignments into subtasks and give each a deadline before the final due date.
  3. Set reminders and review unfinished tasks regularly to adjust your plan.
