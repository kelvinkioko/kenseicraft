How to start a three-entry daily gratitude journal

Write three gratitude entries a day with prompts, mood tracking and reminders.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Gratitude Journal.

Step by step

  1. Pick a regular moment and write three specific things you appreciated today.
  2. Use a prompt if you feel stuck, and add a mood tag to give the entry context.
  3. Return to past entries and use reminders to maintain a routine that works for you.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
