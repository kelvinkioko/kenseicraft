Frequently asked

For collectors who want the numbers.

What cards can MonValue identify?

MonValue is designed for Pokémon, sports and other popular trading-card collections. Clear, well-lit photos produce the strongest matches.

Where do the price estimates come from?

MonValue uses current marketplace data and comparable listings to provide informative market estimates that can change over time.

Can I track my whole collection?

Yes. Save scanned cards to your portfolio, organise important cards and monitor the collection’s estimated value.

Is MonValue free to download?

Yes. MonValue is free to download and offers optional in-app purchases for additional features.

Is MonValue affiliated with card publishers?

No. MonValue is independent and is not affiliated with Pokémon, Nintendo or other card publishers. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
