How to identify a coin from photos

Photograph a coin to identify its country, year, denomination and composition, explore estimated value and rarity, and keep your finds in a digital collection.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Coin Identifier: CoinScope.

Step by step

  1. Photograph both sides of the coin in focus and note any visible lettering, date and mint marks.
  2. Use CoinScope to explore its country, denomination and composition, then compare those details with the coin.
  3. Save the find to your collection. Treat value estimates as a starting point and seek specialist advice for significant items.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
