How to review iPhone photos and videos before deleting them

Find duplicate photos, review large videos, swipe through clutter and clean up contacts and calendar events with a clear, review-first workflow.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Storage Cleaner: CleanKit.

Step by step

  1. Start with a small group of photos or large videos and identify what you no longer need.
  2. Review each proposed deletion in CleanKit and check that important originals have a backup.
  3. Delete only confirmed unwanted items. Check available device storage afterward and review recently deleted items before permanent removal.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
