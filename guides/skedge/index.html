How to plan assignments alongside your weekly schedule

Organize schedules, assignments, subtasks and reminders in one planner.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Skedge.

Step by step

  1. Add your fixed classes, work hours and appointments to a schedule.
  2. Break assignments into subtasks and give each a deadline before the final due date.
  3. Set reminders and review unfinished tasks regularly to adjust your plan.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
