How to prepare and check a client invoice

Manage businesses and clients, create invoices and send them to customers.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Invoice Maker and Receipt.

Step by step

  1. Choose the business issuing the invoice and check the client contact details.
  2. Create the invoice and review its amounts and description against the work agreed with the client.
  3. Check the finished invoice before sending it, and keep a copy for your records.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
