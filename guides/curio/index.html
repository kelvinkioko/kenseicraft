How to research an antique from a photo

Curio identifies antiques, vintage items and collectibles from a photo and provides estimated value, history, rarity and authenticity guidance.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Curio — Antique Identifier: Curator.

Step by step

  1. Photograph the whole item in even light, then take close-ups of maker marks, materials and signs of wear.
  2. Use Curio to explore a possible identification, history and estimated value; compare the result with physical evidence.
  3. Save the item to your collection. For a sale, insurance or a high-value decision, seek a qualified appraisal.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
