How to organize your pantry and plan your next shop

Keep pantry items, quantities and storage locations together with shopping lists and recipes.

This guide walks through a simple workflow using Any Pantry.

Step by step

  1. Add your pantry items, quantities and storage locations so you can see what is already at home.
  2. Review low stock and add needed items to your shopping list; move bought items into your pantry.
  3. Explore meal ideas from your ingredients and check each recipe against what you have before cooking.

Before you finish

Review the result before relying on it. Available controls and features can vary by app version; contact Kensei Craft support if your screen differs from this workflow.
