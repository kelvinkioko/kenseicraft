## 4. What the app does (feature inventory)

1. **Instant photo identification** — camera or photo → name, category, era, origin, maker.
2. **Estimated value & appraisal ranges** — insurance / retail replacement / quick-sale figures.
3. **Authenticity signal** — "likely authentic" confidence %.
4. **Rarity score** — Common → Rare, with a percentage.
5. **Historical context** — era, style, how the object was used, why it matters.
6. **Care & preservation tips** — cleaning and storage guidance per material.
7. **Buyer guidance** — documents to request, questions to ask, ownership costs.
8. **Seller guidance** — recommended listing title, target buyer, best marketplaces.
9. **Collection / cabinet** — save items, track total collection value.
10. **Export & share** — export a discovery as a PDF.
11. Recognises 12+ categories: furniture, jewelry, coins & currency, ceramics/pottery/porcelain,
    paintings & artwork, glassware, watches & clocks, silverware, sculptures, rare collectibles,
    retro décor, historical objects.

## 6. Keyword research (SEO)

Market note: the niche is competitive with several dedicated apps and content sites
(antiqueidentifier.org, theantiqueidentifier.app, lensapp.io, TIQ). The winning play is **not**
head-to-head on the single term "antique identifier app" — it is a **topical cluster** of
high-intent, category- and value-specific long-tail guides that each convert to a download. That is
exactly the guides architecture below.

### 6.1 Primary keywords (page: index.html)

High commercial / download intent. Placed in title, H1, first paragraph, CTA and image alt.

- Unique `<title>` (≤60 chars) + meta description (≤155 chars) per page.
- Canonical URLs on `https://www.kenseicraft.com/curio/`.
